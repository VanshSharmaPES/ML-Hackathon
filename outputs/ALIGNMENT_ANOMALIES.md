Interpretation: a high nearest-reference distance indicates geometric disagreement even before order constraints. A substantially higher monotonic distance indicates that reference order also creates difficulty. Neither diagnostic establishes whether the GPS, reference route or alignment assumptions are wrong; inspect the maps and discuss with organisers.

This explains why a model can have good student-test coverage but much weaker performance on a particular training fold. Retain the documented limitations when reporting model metrics.
